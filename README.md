# Nebula Veil - curiosity through horror
Nebula Veil is the first game of our small team. In it, we want to show what an ordinary person, driven by a thirst for knowledge, can go through. That the motivation behind the desire to unravel the mysteries that stand before him can come from very different sources.

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/92dabc61-c0a8-458a-b4d6-ec3b3e477d5d" style="width:40%; height:auto; display: grid; place-items: center;">

Nebula Veil mixes elements of horror, simulation, puzzle and survival. The player finds himself in the wreckage of a colossal cargo spaceship, trapped in a small tin can called an "engineer vessel". Alone, 9 thousand light years from Earth, with little hope of finding other survivors. While the crash site is enveloped in an ominous blood-red fog.

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b01dd6f9-6bf0-4fe5-b84c-d228be419f5c" style="width:70%; height:auto; display: grid; place-items: center;">

"The fog" creates incredible things. Dangerous, but incredible. These anomalies are difficult to understand, but each of them contains a solution based on physics, logic, or observation. No one will hold the player's hand, but an inventive approach will be rewarded, because each of the anomalies hides an upgrade for the ship and serves as part of the key to the mystery of "The fog".
